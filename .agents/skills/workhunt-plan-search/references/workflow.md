# Plan the search workflow

Read confirmed target roles, capabilities, geography, degree evidence, work preferences, language and requested count. Separate mandatory constraints from preferences and unknowns. Keep the 75% fit gate and fixed source-evidenced manual bounds from explicit private settings; collector, notification, assessment and fixture contexts remain exactly 24 hours. Never widen bounds to reach a count. The shared qualifier applies source-specific windows only when both `run_type` and `execution_context` are `manual`. Read the private `search_brief.manual_source_freshness_hours` and optional `manual_freshness_policy`; shared defaults are empty. At a new run, fix one anchor instant, subtract elapsed hours in UTC and express bounds in the configured IANA time zone, including clock changes. Save the bounds, time zone, source windows, policy and requested minimum together. A configured `employer_registry` or `techmap` exception requires the exact opportunity ID and listing URL paired in one identity or explicit alias, a public provenance URL, textual membership evidence and an aware, nonfuture observation time. A later membership observation does not move the publication anchor. Unsupported membership falls back to that saved run's `other` window, normally 24 hours for a new run; discovery/feed update times and a cross-post alone do not prove membership or freshness. Saved bounds and policy remain authoritative when the current profile changes. Collector, notification, assessment and fixture contexts always use exactly 24 elapsed hours, including legacy scan runs. Settings do not authorize execution. Choose source groups by access, language, work type and eligibility, without enabling them. Reference exact history suppressions rather than inventing a standard list. Produce a reusable search brief with supported criteria, source choices, authorization boundary and unresolved dependencies. No scripts, network execution, account connection, schedule, message or application follows from planning.
