# Evidence-based opportunity research

## Candidate and authorization

Load the private profile and evidence ledger in `.workhunt/`. Configure them from the actual selected CV using `workhunt-configure-profile`; never reconstruct a candidate from a public skill example. Career facts do not establish desired roles, geography, work authorization, language proficiency, availability, rates or relocation preferences. Unknowns stay empty and conflicts stay visible. Read the current user instruction for scope; research is not submission authorization.

## Search and source selection

Read both canonical history files before searching. Fix an ISO timestamp window in the configured time zone using the manual policy below, or exactly 24 hours for automated qualification. Keep the user's requested minimum and actual count separate; a minimum is not a cap. Select sources from all 28 records in `sources/opportunity-sources.json` using confirmed language, geography, acquisition route and work preferences. The catalog contains dated reference evidence, not enabled integrations or current availability guarantees. Public listing collectors require explicit opt-in. Use an isolated hidden browser or public page tools, never personal browser state.

The shared qualifier applies source-specific windows only when both `run_type` and `execution_context` are `manual`. Read the private `search_brief.manual_source_freshness_hours` and optional `manual_freshness_policy`; shared defaults are empty. At a new run, fix one anchor instant, subtract elapsed hours in UTC and express bounds in the configured IANA time zone, including clock changes. Save the bounds, time zone, source windows, policy and requested minimum together. A configured `employer_registry` or `techmap` exception requires the exact opportunity ID and listing URL paired in one identity or explicit alias, a public provenance URL, textual membership evidence and an aware, nonfuture observation time. A later membership observation does not move the publication anchor. Unsupported membership falls back to that saved run's `other` window, normally 24 hours for a new run; discovery/feed update times and a cross-post alone do not prove membership or freshness. Saved bounds and policy remain authoritative when the current profile changes. Collector, notification, assessment and fixture contexts always use exactly 24 elapsed hours, including legacy scan runs. Settings do not authorize execution.

Loading historical data is distinct from creating a new profile brief. Only a new profile brief or a saved snapshot explicitly marked `execution_context: manual` may derive source windows from preferences. For an exact copied legacy brief, recover context only from unambiguous canonical snapshots with consistent context, policy, time zone and bounds. Inferred manual context alone does not activate dormant mappings. Unknown or conflicting manual history stays `unresolved` at its recorded base bounds; supplied source-window evidence is retained separately as `recorded_source_windows`. Keep `context_provenance` and matching run IDs inside private records/packets. Preserve original provenance and `recorded_source_windows` through normalization, JSON save/reload and packet preparation; `context_resolution` describes the current normalization separately. Neither provenance field grants permission or activates preferences. Do not invent manual markers or silently replace contradictory supplied bounds. Explicit collector, notification, assessment and fixture behavior remains 24 hours.

## Fit and eligibility

Read the entire vacancy or client brief. Count each distinct mandatory skill/experience criterion once, including unverified ones in the denominator. Supported evidence earns one point; 75% exactly passes the default fit threshold. Show supported/total, unrounded decision and gaps. A single ordinary gap is not a veto. Separately require the configured role, capabilities and confirmed eligibility to work from an explicitly configured country. Remote is not worldwide. Work mode, language, office access and time-zone restrictions remain evidence fields.

A mandatory degree must be supported by an actually completed qualifying credential or the advertisement's accepted equivalent. Studies are not a degree. Record mandatory unmet, mandatory met, experience-equivalent, preferred, unstated or ambiguous distinctly. Where the private profile records a supported technology-duration limit, an explicit requirement exceeding it without an accepted alternative is a conflict. Never invent versions, tenure or proficiency. Unknown duration earns no duration claim.

## Dates, routes and identity

Record original publication, refresh/repost, feed availability, first discovery, deadline and verification separately. A genuine evidenced refresh/repost can satisfy the applicable fixed window even if original publication is older. Relative/date-only evidence qualifies only when its entire supported interval is inside the window. A crawl timestamp or alert receipt does not establish freshness.

Open the exact application/proposal route and match employer plus title/ID. Record check time, evidence, route URL/navigation, message-field availability and limits. A current vacancy-specific acceptance statement behind login may qualify with `Login required; not verified beyond this point`; a generic login page, email address or unrelated form does not. Stop before entering personal information unless separately authorized.

Deduplicate employer/ATS/requisition or platform/project identities and supplier cross-posts. A repost never clears suppressions or previously reported state. Missing history is unknown. Previously discovered but never reported opportunities may qualify after current checks. Preserve all provenance and first-seen observations.

## Cards, drafts and records

Report exact title/ID, listing and application/proposal URL, city/country, work mode, eligibility, publication event, route-check time, supported/total fit and gaps in the configured language. Include requested platform-specific drafts locally, clearly `Draft — not sent`; never invent a message field, recipient, attachment, rate, availability or submission. No recommendation draft for an excluded/stale item unless separately requested as a clarification draft outside the count.

Records are `.workhunt/data/jobs.json` and `.workhunt/data/applied_jobs.json`. Record actual coverage and shortfall without weakening gates. Read history again before reporting. An explicit manual search gets an honest result even at zero. Only an already-authorized notification workflow may require silence at zero; do not create one implicitly.

## CVs, applications and outcomes

Select the exact CV by role/language from private `cv-manifest.json`, preserving originals and unresolved chronology. Existence/hash matches do not prove factual correctness. Application packets remain local and unsent. Actual uploading, bidding, payments, messaging and submission require direct bounded authorization. Record prepared, uploaded, observed submission, receipt, user-reported submission and recruiter response separately with timestamps/evidence. A receipt is not recruiter interest; drafting is not sending.

## Presentation and project work

Only when requested, curate portfolio evidence, draft positioning or founder offers, or plan discoverability. Attribute individual, team, upstream and AI-assisted contributions. A storyboard is not a demonstrated run; a claimed metric needs evidence. Keep private/restricted material out of public drafts. Service definition does not authorize implementation, pricing, commitments or publication.

Read the current private `work_location_policy` separately from saved freshness: explicit `base_country`, `work_path`, selected `exclude_required_foreign_conditions` and `unknown_eligibility` handling. Unconfigured policy has no country fallback. Eligibility evidence must match both country and path, pair the exact opportunity ID and listing URL, record an aware nonfuture observation and review the full ad or a verified form linked from that listing. Required conflicting foreign conditions exclude; absent, ambiguous or contradictory evidence cannot qualify. Optional alternatives remain separate, and travel uncertainty does not imply a visa requirement. Publisher nationality, remote/sponsorship labels and CV location establish neither citizenship/permits nor eligibility. Full-ad absence of a requirement is limited evidence, not employer confirmation or legal clearance. Apply current preferences in scan, render and packets, including saved run-ID and copied-brief requests, while preserving historical windows, minimums, source policy and saved decisions. Store actual policy provenance and eligibility assessments only in private records, packets and pages.
