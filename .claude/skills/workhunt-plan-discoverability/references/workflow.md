# Plan discoverability workflow

Read the requested channels, target audience, supported facts and explicit language/geography preferences. Inspect only authorized content; attachment-reported issues remain attributed until checked. Prepare content briefs with intended reader action, public-safe proof, ownership and unresolved facts. Keep employer and founder entry points coherent without inventing market demand. Propose language/geography choices only from confirmed proficiency and eligibility. Define manual outcome records connecting an exact asset/version, observed or user-reported event, date and limits; views are not applications, inquiries are not contracts and correlation is not causation. A content plan does not authorize profile edits, website work, analytics installation, marketing automation, account changes or outreach.
