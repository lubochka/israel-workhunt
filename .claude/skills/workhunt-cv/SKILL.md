# Prepare a truthful CV

Read `references/context.md` and `references/playbook.md`. Select the exact role/language CV using the private manifest and read its contents. A hash or filename is not factual reconciliation. Preserve originals, dates, role titles, contributions, credential completion and conflicts. Adapt emphasis only within the requested CV task; never invent capabilities, versions, duration, proficiency or metrics. Keep drafts and extracts in `.workhunt/`. Do not upload, publish or submit; selection alone is not attachment or delivery.
