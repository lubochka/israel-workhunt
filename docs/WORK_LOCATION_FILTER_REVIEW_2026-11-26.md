# Work-location evidence review

This English release note preserves the design conclusions from source work dated 2026-11-26. It is not a new live provider, vacancy, application-form or legal review. Actual opportunity assessments, personal instructions and observed evidence remain private.

[TheRemoteFreelancer](https://github.com/engineerapart/TheRemoteFreelancer) prompted research into per-opportunity work-location restrictions. Its pinned [Unlicense](https://github.com/engineerapart/TheRemoteFreelancer/blob/849f6c6c3b080bfbf9cd0722e72a8cfcf5aee407/LICENSE) is separate from Workhunt's AGPL-3.0-only license. This implementation imports no catalog or assets from that project. The 23-source catalog and pinned 288-board integration are unchanged.

Platform coverage, a publisher's nationality, a remote label, or a sponsorship question cannot establish that a particular country and work path is permitted. Assess the full exact advertisement and any verified form linked from it. Preserve paired listing identity/URL, actual source URL, textual evidence and an aware observation time. A generic country page or an unrelated form is insufficient.

Apply the user's current explicitly configured work-location policy independently of saved freshness. Evidence must match both its base country and work path. Exclude evidenced mandatory foreign conditions selected by that policy. Missing, ambiguous, contradictory or differently scoped evidence remains unresolved. Distinguish optional alternatives from mandatory conditions; an office visit does not by itself prove a visa requirement.

The absence of a foreign condition in the full advertisement is limited evidence, not employer confirmation. An attributed employer confirmation is still not a determination of candidate citizenship, permits or legal clearance. Domestic conditions do not establish legal status. Work-location preferences come from explicit user choices; CV residence, employer country and absent facts do not supply those choices.

Saved run windows, source-policy snapshots, minimums and historical decisions remain unchanged when current work-location policy changes. The current qualification result is separate from the saved result. All resulting eligibility evidence in records, packets and rendered pages belongs inside the private workspace.
