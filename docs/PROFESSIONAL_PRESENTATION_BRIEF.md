# Professional presentation brief

Use only supplied or explicitly selected CV and portfolio evidence. State the requested audience, artifact, channel and language. Preserve chronology, contributions, credential completion, technology versions and measured outcomes with source locators. Missing or conflicting facts stay explicit.

Employer-facing positioning connects supported work to role needs. Founder-facing positioning connects supported capabilities to a client outcome, deliverables, exclusions and unresolved commercial terms. Neither audience changes the underlying facts. No invented tenure, metrics, preference, availability, rate or production claim.

Drafts remain private under `.workhunt/`. Curating a sample or planning a demo does not authorize running code, changing repositories, publishing, uploading, account enrollment or outreach. Keep actual verification separate from a proposed storyboard and historical observations.
