# Assess fit workflow

Read the complete advertisement. Enumerate distinct mandatory requirements once; supported evidence is 1 and unmet/unverified evidence is 0, retained in the denominator. Decide at 75% exactly without rounding and show M/N, percentage, evidence and gaps. Check the configured roles and capabilities separately. A mandatory degree requires a completed qualifying credential or an accepted equivalent; studies alone are not completion. Apply profile-specific supported skill-duration limits only to explicit longer requirements without a met alternative. Do not invent versions, proficiency or tenure. Confirm eligibility from a configured country; remote alone is insufficient. Projects also need scope, commercial terms and deadlines recorded. A fit score is separate from freshness, route and history, so a passing CV match may still be outside the verified shortlist. No CV rewrite, application or broader search is implicit.
