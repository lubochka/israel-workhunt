# Plan the search workflow

Read confirmed target roles, capabilities, geography, degree evidence, work preferences, language and requested count. Separate mandatory constraints from preferences and unknowns. Keep the 75% fit gate and fixed source-evidenced manual bounds from explicit private settings; collector, notification, assessment and fixture contexts remain exactly 24 hours. Never widen bounds to reach a count. The shared qualifier applies source-specific windows only when both `run_type` and `execution_context` are `manual`. Read the private `search_brief.manual_source_freshness_hours` and optional `manual_freshness_policy`; shared defaults are empty. At a new run, fix one anchor instant, subtract elapsed hours in UTC and express bounds in the configured IANA time zone, including clock changes. Save the bounds, time zone, source windows, policy and requested minimum together. A configured `employer_registry` or `techmap` exception requires the exact opportunity ID and listing URL paired in one identity or explicit alias, a public provenance URL, textual membership evidence and an aware, nonfuture observation time. A later membership observation does not move the publication anchor. Unsupported membership falls back to that saved run's `other` window, normally 24 hours for a new run; discovery/feed update times and a cross-post alone do not prove membership or freshness. Saved bounds and policy remain authoritative when the current profile changes. Collector, notification, assessment and fixture contexts always use exactly 24 elapsed hours, including legacy scan runs. Settings do not authorize execution.

Loading historical data is distinct from creating a new profile brief. Only a new profile brief or a saved snapshot explicitly marked `execution_context: manual` may derive source windows from preferences. For an exact copied legacy brief, recover context only from unambiguous canonical snapshots with consistent context, policy, time zone and bounds. Inferred manual context alone does not activate dormant mappings. Unknown or conflicting manual history stays `unresolved` at its recorded base bounds; supplied source-window evidence is retained separately as `recorded_source_windows`. Keep `context_provenance` and matching run IDs inside private records/packets. Preserve original provenance and `recorded_source_windows` through normalization, JSON save/reload and packet preparation; `context_resolution` describes the current normalization separately. Neither provenance field grants permission or activates preferences. Do not invent manual markers or silently replace contradictory supplied bounds. Explicit collector, notification, assessment and fixture behavior remains 24 hours. Choose source groups by access, language, work type and eligibility, without enabling them. Reference exact history suppressions rather than inventing a standard list. Produce a reusable search brief with supported criteria, source choices, authorization boundary and unresolved dependencies. No scripts, network execution, account connection, schedule, message or application follows from planning.

Read the current private `work_location_policy` separately from saved freshness: explicit `base_country`, `work_path`, selected `exclude_required_foreign_conditions` and `unknown_eligibility` handling. Unconfigured policy has no country fallback. Eligibility evidence must match both country and path, pair the exact opportunity ID and listing URL, record an aware nonfuture observation and review the full ad or a verified form linked from that listing. Required conflicting foreign conditions exclude; absent, ambiguous or contradictory evidence cannot qualify. Optional alternatives remain separate, and travel uncertainty does not imply a visa requirement. Publisher nationality, remote/sponsorship labels and CV location establish neither citizenship/permits nor eligibility. Full-ad absence of a requirement is limited evidence, not employer confirmation or legal clearance. Apply current preferences in scan, render and packets, including saved run-ID and copied-brief requests, while preserving historical windows, minimums, source policy and saved decisions. Store actual policy provenance and eligibility assessments only in private records, packets and pages.

Keep employment `required_stack` separate from explicitly confirmed client-project alternatives in `search_brief.project_stack_policy`. The empty shared policy enables none; CV technologies never adopt preferences. Search each selected project capability and client-outcome term independently of employment combinations. Generic titles and incomplete descriptions may be provisional leads, but alternate-stack qualification needs paid buyer deliverables, matching configured capability keys and exact listing evidence. Only actual buyer requirements enter the unchanged 75% score, including backend gaps. Preserve every independent eligibility, degree, freshness, route and history gate. Hidden buyer names remain unknown when an exact project ID identifies the brief. Saved project policies, including absence, never inherit today's choices.

For an explicit manual report, show new promising projects within the selected policy with evidenced fit of at least 75% but unresolved independent checks as separate unverified notes: score/gaps, exact link, missing evidence and next check. They count zero qualified matches and receive no recommendation letter. This does not add a renderer feature, change notification silence or create an alert.
