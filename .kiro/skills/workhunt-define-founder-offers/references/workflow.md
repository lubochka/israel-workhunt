# Define founder offers workflow

Identify the client's users, workflow, product stage and intended outcome. For a full offer set, consider four optional forms: discovery/technical assessment, bounded prototype, scoped implementation increment, and ongoing technical guidance. Use only forms supported by evidence; do not infer that every candidate can offer all four. State deliverables, exclusions, dependencies, responsibilities, acceptance discussions and relevant proof. Separate assessment from later execution; give proportionate architecture when relevant. Rates, dates, legal terms, estimates and availability stay unknown unless explicitly supplied. A real brief still needs fit/date/route/history checks. Draft a plain-language engagement process and unsent inquiry prompt. No quote, negotiation, client contact, bid, agreement, implementation or deployment.
