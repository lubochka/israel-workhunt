# Verify the response route workflow

During an authorized exact-link check, follow the real Apply/Proposal navigation and match the destination to employer/title/ID. Record exact source/final URLs, identity, timestamp, current form status and observed message-field labels/limits/questions. Unknown post-login fields stay unknown. A login exception requires current evidence this exact vacancy accepts applications and the label `Login required; not verified beyond this point`. Generic login, email-only advertisements, general inquiry forms and unrelated forms fail the specific-route gate. Distinguish technical blockage from employer-confirmed closure. For projects retain fees, eligibility and proposal conditions. Stop before entering personal data, uploads, bids or sending. Never invent an alternative recipient or imply final acceptance from a visible form.
